Movember Fantasy Football

2026/27 FPL challenge, gameweeks 9 to 12

    How it works

    • Scoring follows the official FPL points rules, as a four-gameweek sprint from GW9 to GW12.
    • Pick a starting eleven in a 4-4-2: one goalkeeper, four defenders, four midfielders and two forwards.
    • Set and forget. No substitutions or transfers. If a player is injured or doesn’t play, you play with one fewer.
    • No budget. Pick whoever you like.
    • Only one player from each Premier League club.
    • Pick one captain for all four gameweeks. Your captain scores double every week and can’t be changed, even if injured.
    • Positions follow the official FPL position allocation.
    • A postponed game that’s replayed within GW9 to GW12 counts as a double gameweek. Only games played in GW9 to GW12 count.
    • Squads stay hidden until the game starts on 1 November. You can change your team until then.

    Entry fee

    • Entry is $20 per team: $10 goes to Movember and $10 goes into the prize pool.

    Prize

    • The team with the highest points total across GW9 to GW12 wins the whole prize pool — winner takes all.
    • If there’s a tie for first place, the highest scorer in GW12 wins. If it’s still a draw, the highest score in GW11, then GW10, then GW9 decides it, whichever produces a winner first. If it’s still a draw after that, the prize is shared.
    • A league table goes round after the final match of each gameweek.
